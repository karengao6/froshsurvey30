About the survey

Welcome to the
Orange Bubble.

Welcome to The Daily Princetonian's fifth annual survey of the freshman class. The data within this project tell a bit of the story of the Great Class of 2030.

Our project seeks to dive into a holistic profile of the University's youngest class. While the University publicly reports demographic and academic information, other data — such as drinking habits, relationships, or intentions to make a lot of money — are harder to find publicly.

We invite you to browse our findings with a curious mindset. Click on every graph, select every dropdown, and really get to know the fresh '30s.

To the Class of 2030, thank you to each member of your class who filled out our survey, telling their unique story. We hope you enjoy clicking through the various visualizations as much as we did.

Best,
Vincent Etherton '26 and Alexa Wingate '27
